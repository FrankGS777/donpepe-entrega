# Industrial surfaces

These local assets are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The application does not call their API.

Original asset pages, download URLs, exact byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes are
recorded in `manifest.json`. The JPG maps retain their native 2048 × 2048 pixels;
the HDR environments retain their native 1024 × 512, 2048 × 1024 and
4096 × 2048 pixels as listed below. No upscaling is claimed.

- concrete_floor_02: diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness
- brown_planks_03: diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness
- metal_plate: diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness (metal paint retains authored colour)
- asphalt_03: diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness
- industrial_sunset_02: Radiance HDR environment
- empty_warehouse_01: interior Radiance HDR, 2048 × 1024, Sergej Majboroda,
  [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/empty_warehouse_01), CC0 1.0. Used for the
  actual lighting and metal reflections, never as a substitute for scene geometry.
- industrial_sunset_02_puresky: Radiance HDR, 4096 × 2048, Jarod Guest and
  Sergej Majboroda, [source](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_sunset_02_puresky),
  CC0 1.0. The sky-only panorama avoids importing a foreign landscape into
  the Lima scenery. Terrain and buildings remain real scene meshes.

The procedural surfaces, machinery geometry, articulation, UI and business
simulation in DonPepe are separate project resources.
